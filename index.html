How to play

    
The overworld

    
Walk with the arrow keys, WASD or the on-screen arrows. Walk into a zombie to fight it. Walk into Crazy Dave (D) to shop or practice, into a chest (C) to open it, and into a sign to read it. Beat every zombie in a chapter and the boss (B) will fight you. Beat the boss to open the gate (G) to the next chapter.

    
Battles

    
Your team of up to 5 plants fights waves of zombies. Every round, each plant and zombie gets one turn, fastest first. On a plant's turn, pick a move (click it or press 1-4), then click the target.

    
Sun is shared by the whole team. You get some every round. Basic moves are free; stronger ones cost sun, and many need a few turns to recharge. Soak up sun (5) skips a turn for +15 sun. Plant Food (F) makes a plant's next move free and twice as strong.

    
Armor (cones, buckets, helmets) soaks up damage first. Moves that pierce go straight through. Lobbed moves fly over Screen Doors and reach diving zombies. Every plant has its own ability: read it on the Team screen.

    
Classes

    
Shooters are all-rounders. Hearty plants are tanks. Sunny plants make sun and heal. Volatile plants deal burst damage. Growers are gimmicky plants that need a plan.

    
Gimmicks

    
Many battles have a gimmick that changes the rules: Night, Fog, Pool, Roof, Gravestones, Drought, Conveyor Belt, Storm and more. They're shown at the top of the battle and in the Almanac.

    
Versus

    
Two teams of plants fight each other: you against the computer (Easy, Normal or Hard), two people taking turns on one screen, or the computer against itself. Every plant is unlocked at level 15. Each side has its own sun, and you can pick a gimmick and how much Plant Food each side gets. Versus never changes your story save.

    
Growing

    
Plants earn XP and level up, learning new moves, up to level 15: every plant learns a big move at level 10 and another at level 13, and gets a second ability at level 15. Plants on the bench get some XP too. Unlock new plants by winning story battles, beating rogue plants, shopping with Crazy Dave, and earning tough achievements. Once you beat a world's boss, you can refight any of its battles: step on a dim letter on the map.
