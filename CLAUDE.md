## What this is

**Lawn Legends** is a free, browser-based **Plants vs. Zombies fan RPG**: turn-based battles drawn in ASCII art, a walkable ASCII overworld, a story mode, a team of five, classes, customizable movesets, unlockable plants, battle gimmicks and achievements, and a versus mode (plants vs plants). ## How the game works

- **Story mode:** 6 chapters, each a walkable ASCII map (`CHAPTERS` in `world.js`): Front Lawn (Day), Night Backyard (Night), The Pool, The Roof, Zomboss's Lair, then the post-game **Time Rift** with characters from across the series (Egypt, Wild West, Pirate Seas, Dark Ages, Frostbite Caves, Neon Mixtape Tour, Jurassic Marsh, Far Future). Walk with arrows/WASD/the on-screen pad. Bumping into a battle tile starts it. Battles block the path until beaten. The boss (`B`) only fights once every non-optional battle is won (`bossReady`); rogue plants (`R`/`Q`) and the secret Yeti (drawn as a tree) are optional. Beating the boss opens the gate (`G`). **Refights** (owner, 2026-11-22: "once you beat a world you can infinitely refight the zombies there"): a beaten battle is plain floor until its chapter's boss is beaten; then every beaten battle in that chapter (boss, rogues and the Yeti too, drawn `Y`) comes back as a dim letter (`k-done` in `tileLook`). Stepping onto one moves you there and opens the prebattle box as a "Rematch" (`moveWorld`); "Not yet" leaves you standing on it, so paths never get blocked. A rematch passes the normal battle key, so `winBattle` gives the usual coins and XP but no first-win prize and no story ending again. Crazy Dave (`D`) opens the shop, practice battles and tips. Chests (`C`) give coins and Plant Food.
- **Battles** (`battle.js`): your team (up to 5) against waves of up to 5 enemies. Each round every unit acts once, fastest first. Plants: pick one of 4 equipped moves and a target. **Sun** is shared: `SUN_START` 50, `SUN_INCOME` 25 per round, basic moves free, big moves cost sun and have cooldowns. "Soak up sun" skips a turn for +15. **Plant Food** (bought for 60, max 5) makes a plant's next move free and x2. Plants fully heal after every battle (no attrition) and heal 20% between waves. Losing gives half XP; running away gives nothing.
- **Classes** (owner's names): Shooters (all-rounders), Hearty (tanks), Sunny (support), Volatile (burst damage), Growers (gimmicks needing strategy). Every plant learns 6 moves by level and equips any 4 on the Team screen and **a 7th move at Lv10** (2026-11-22, owner: "give every character a level 10 actually really strong move or just something that ties the kit together"). Wall-nut's is Wall-nut Bowling (bounces into up to 3 more enemies); others include Solar Flare (spends all sun as damage), Golden Age (every ally gets an extra turn), Kamikaze Kaboom (Cherry Bomb wilts, Last Blast also fires), Mega Chomp (swallows any non-boss), Shatter (hits chilled/frozen enemies), Glacier, Full Broadside (`pre` hook loads Coconut first), Spikerock and Second Burst (once per battle: cooldown `ONCE_PER_BATTLE` 99, shown as "once per battle"). Look-alike moves got kit twists the same day ("make individual characters a little more different"): Wind Up (next Bloomerang throw bounces to everyone), Charge Up (next Laser Bean beam at full power), Garlic Bulb (heals 15% per Weak enemy), Caltrops, Gourd Guard, Cabbage Patch, Spore Burst, Golden Glint, Bean Shine, Confuse, Sandstorm Spines, Lock On, Hologram Lure, Attract. **Every move must fit the plant** (owner, 2026-11-21: "why does squash have so many cherry bomb moves"): there are no class-wide move pools anymore. Most moves belong to one plant; a few "family moves" are shared, but only by plants of the same family ("some moves can be kept between plants of the same line but not all"): peas (Pea Volley, Take Aim), pults (Arc Shot), nuts (Shell Up, Nut Wall), flowers (Bloom, Second Bloom), mushrooms (Spore Cloud), icy plants (Frostbite), fire plants (Ignite), explosives (Short Fuse, Kaboom), Far Future plants (Overclock). The themed moves copy the strength of the class moves they replaced, so balance didn't change. Then, on request ("change what some moves do as well so it is made different", "replace a few moves so they can have completely different uses"), about 25 moves got brand-new effects: Wall-nut's Block for You and Tall-nut's Tall Shadow make them take hits for allies (`cover`), Marigold's Golden Hour gives an ally an extra turn, Sunflower's Pep Talk clears an ally's cooldowns, Laser Bean's Recalibrate clears its own, Hypno-shroom's Lullaby puts a zombie to sleep until hit, E.M.Peach's Disable silences specials, Gold Bloom's Gleam blinds, Fume-shroom's Corrode marks (+30% damage taken), Citron's Bounty pays sun, Garlic's Ward blocks bad statuses, Bonk Choy's Rope-a-dope counters, Squash's "Hmm..." executes (x3 under 40% HP), Chomper's Gobble steals life, Aloe's Sap Share trades HP, Jalapeno's Flare Up and Snapdragon's Stoke work on burning enemies, Torchwood's Kindling makes an ally fiery, Ice-shroom's Cryo Sleep heals but freezes itself, Iceberg's Brain Freeze strips buffs, Salad Bar / Spore Glow give Regen, Hide Under Cap gives Evade. Saved equipped moves that no longer exist are filled in by `equipped()` in `save.js` without changing the save.
- **Every plant has a passive ability** (owner's request), as hooks: `roundStart`, `waveStart`, `dealMult`, `takeMult`, `onHurt`, `onFall`, `onAllyFall`, `afterAttack`, `onDevour`, `onStatus`, `turnStart`, plus flags (`lobAll`, `pierceAll`, `dodge`, `dodgeStraight`, `reflect`, `echo`, `ricochet`, `beam`, `scorch`, `splashAll`, `torch`, `icyBonus`, `healBoost`, `coinBonus`, `butter`, `blocksVault`, `noStun`, `emp`, `immune`).
- **Stat budget (no strict upgrades):** `HP / 8 + ATK + DEF + SPD` is 30–40 for every plant; plants with a strong passive sit lower. Example pairs: Repeater vs Peashooter (more hits, slower, no Steady Aim), Tall-nut vs Wall-nut (tougher, slower, no Tough Shell), Melon-pult vs Cabbage-pult (splash, slowest), Laser Bean (hits everyone at half power, never crits). **Pumpkin was toned down** (2026-11-22, owner: "tone it down"): a test that swaps one plant for a random other with the same 4 teammates showed Pumpkin added about +12 points to a team (Squash +7, Wall-nut +3), almost all from its every-round free shield. That shield went from 8% to 4% of Pumpkin's max HP, bringing Pumpkin to about +6. **Moves follow the same rule** (owner, 2026-11-22: "isn't frostbite objectively worse than frozen pea"): no move may be strictly worse than another move the same plant learns. A scan (same target, more or equal damage, less or equal cost and cooldown, at least the same status, no upside lost) found four and they were fixed: Frostbite now does triple damage to chilled or frozen enemies (`vsChilled`), Bounce hits a second enemy (`foe2`), Stink Storm makes everyone Weak and Marked, Petal Spin costs 25. Re-run that scan after adding moves.
- **Mechanics:** new statuses (2026-11-21): `marked`, `bounty`, `blind`, `sleep`, `silence`, `regen`, `counter`, `cover` (with `coverBy`), `ward`, `evade`, `fiery`; numbers in `config.js` (`MARK_MULT`, `EVADE_CHANCE`, `REGEN_PART`, `COUNTER_POWER`, `BOUNTY_SUN`). Rogue plants' heals and shields are worked out from their normal HP, not their tripled HP (a rogue Spikeweed could drag a fight on for 200 rounds). armor soaks damage before HP unless the attack **pierces**; **lobbed** attacks skip Screen Door armor and reach diving Snorkels; Torchwood sets allies' straight shots on fire; fire melts chill/freeze; **burning units can't be healed** (counter to Scientist); vault/leap/swing attacks ignore Taunt but must hit a Tall-nut; bosses can't be devoured or hypnotized and resist stuns 50% (E.M.Peach's stuns always work on machines). Statuses last a number of the affected unit's own turns; a unit buffing itself gets +1 so the buff covers its next turn.
- **Battle gimmicks** (`GIMMICKS` in `data.js`; owner asked for them twice): Day, Night, Pool, Roof, Fog, Gravestones, Drought, Last Stand, Conveyor Belt, Storm, Rush, Heat Wave, Sandstorm, Freezing Wind, Neon Jams, Time Warp. Each chapter has one; battles can add more.
- **Unlocks** (40 plants: 3 starters, Peashooter, Sunflower and Wall-nut; the other 37 are unlocked): story battles (`reward.plant`), rogue plants beaten in the overworld (owner's idea), Crazy Dave's shop, and tough / secret / tough-collection achievements (owner rejected "100-damage hit" as an unlock; Squash = Untouchable, Cactus = Seed Vault (own 20), Gold Bloom = secret Sunny Side Up), and the three added on 2026-11-21 are all achievement rewards (owner's choice): Lightning Reed = Storm Chaser (win a Storm battle with no plant wilting), Magnet-shroom = Scrap Collector (break 30 armor pieces, `stats.armorBroken`), Umbrella Leaf = Rejected! (defeat 10 Catapult Zombies, `stats.catapults`). New plants join one level below your best plant.
- **Colors and designs:** every plant and zombie drawing is painted character by character in its real colors (`DRAWINGS` in `art.js`: `art` is the drawing, `ink` a same-shaped map of palette letters, `pal` the colors; a space in the ink means color `a`; `spriteHtml` turns it into HTML). Requested in two steps ("change the colors of each plant to be accurate", then "look up characters to truly see their accurate color pallet and design"). On 2026-11-21 every character was checked against its picture on plantsvszombies.wiki.gg (fandom.com blocks automated reads). Corrections found that way: Marigold has white petals and a yellow face, Bloomerang is white with a blue center, Infi-nut is a cyan hologram on a projector base, Snapdragon is an olive dragon with an orange-red crest, Squash is pale yellow-green, Spikeweed has pale spikes on a brown base, the Flag Zombie's flag is red, the Dancing Zombie's afro is orange-brown, the Swashbuckler dresses in black. **Eyes and anything black use light or mid-grey colors** because the background is dark and black text would vanish. The class color is still used for a plant's name and card border.
- **Levels don't raise stats** (owner, 2026-11-22: "remove stats getting better as levels increase and instead have the starting stats be around level 7, make the max level 10", "make the zombies also level locked in strength"). `statScale(side)` in `config.js`: your plants always get `PLANT_STAT_SCALE` 1.72 (old level 7), enemies (zombies and rogue plants) always `ENEMY_STAT_SCALE` 1.5 (about old level 5). With both at 1.72 the bot lost much more (plants used to sit ~2 levels above the zombies). Levels (max 10) only unlock moves. Story enemy levels were squeezed from 1–17 into 1–10 (`round(old × 10 / 17)`) and now only matter for XP and coins. Because players can't grind past a wall anymore, Assembly Line (Lair) lost its Gargantuar after a simulated playthrough got stuck there. That playthrough (simple bot) then finished everything; plants reach Lv10 around the Lair. Boss win rates for random level-9 teams: Roof 5/6, Assembly Line 5/6, Dr. Zomboss 3/6, Tomorrow-tron 5/6.
- **Leveling:** XP per beaten enemy is `6 + 2 × level` (×3 for bosses), shared with the whole team (bench gets 40%), and scaled by `xpFactor` so plants above the enemies' level learn less.
- **Zombie variety** (owner, 2026-11-22: "add more zombie variety"): 13 more zombies, each checked against its wiki picture and each with a counter: Ladder (while it has its ladder, all zombies ignore Taunt: `ladderUp` in `battle.js`), Balloon (25% damage until a pierce or lob pops it), Pogo (vaults while it has its pogo stick), Zombie Bobsled Team (vehicle, double damage to chilled plants), Troglobite (fire melts its whole ice block), Prospector (once-per-battle dynamite jump; burning blows it up), Chicken Wrangler (releases 3 Zombie Chickens when first hit), Octo (wraps a plant 2 turns), Boombox (stuns the team while its boombox is intact), Barrel Roller (Imps jump out of the broken barrel), Foot Soldier (Evade every other round), Pharaoh (huge sarcophagus). They're mixed into Night, Pool, Roof, Lair and Time Rift battles and the practice pools. `takeMult` and `onHurt` now get the real `fire`/`pierce`/`lob` of the hit (Torchwood fire counts).
- **Bosses:** boss zombies (`'name!'`) get `BOSS_HP` (2×) health; rogue plants get `ROGUE_HP` (3×). Dr. Zomboss and Tomorrow-tron were eased after testing: summons call 1 zombie every 5 turns, fury is Power for 3 turns (not permanent), and their first waves are lighter.
- **Achievements:** 28 in `ACHIEVEMENTS` (`world.js`), all shown with their name, how to earn them and their reward. Five used to be `secret` ("???" until earned); the owner asked on 2026-11-22 for "the secret achievements [to be] no longer secret", so the flag and the hiding were removed. Awarded with `award(id)` from `battle.js`, `save.js` and `screens.js`.
- **Versus mode** (`versus.js`, 2026-11-22, owner: "a versus ai mode where both sides get teams of plants with still custom movesets and battle each other, you can choose either to fight an ai, fight someone else, or to have 2 ai fight each other and everything is unlocked for this mode"). The owner's choices: two players share **one computer** (no online play, there's no server), a **gimmick picker** (None, Random, or one of `VS_GIMMICKS`: the zombie-only ones, Gravestones, Rush, Sandstorm and Neon Jams, are left out), **Plant Food per side** Off/1/2/3 (owner: "toggleable"; free, never from the story stock), and **Easy/Normal/Hard** AI per AI side. Every plant is available at Lv10 with all 7 moves; each team picks its own 4 moves per plant (the same plant can have different moves on the two teams). Teams and settings are saved under their own key, `lawnlegends:versus`, never in `progress`; versus gives no XP, coins, stats or achievements (`award()` returns early while `B.versus` is set, and story counters use `mine(u)` in `battle.js`). How it works: side `ally` is Player 1 (left), `foe` is Player 2 (right); `B.versus` holds who controls each side (`ctrl`), the AI difficulty, each side's Plant Food and the side names; each side has its own sun in `B.suns` (story mode uses `B.suns.ally` only); both teams use `PLANT_STAT_SCALE`; plant rules that only worked for your team (Torchwood, Umbrella Leaf, fog misses, Hypno-shroom's hypnotize, Infi-nut rebuilding, Bounty, allies-fall hooks, Freezing Wind) work for both teams in versus (`|| B.versus` checks). **Overtime** (my addition, because two healing/shielding teams stalled for 11,000+ rounds in a test): after round `VS_OVERTIME_ROUND` (30) every plant wilts 4% of max HP more each round (`VS_OVERTIME_PART`), so every battle ends; both teams wilting at once is a draw. **The versus AI** (`versusAct`) scores every move on every target in "HP" (damage capped at what the target can take, a bonus for knock-outs, rough worth of statuses/heals/shields/revives, minus sun cost at `AI_SUN_VALUE` 0.4 per sun) and can plan Plant Food; Easy picks a random affordable move, Normal adds noise and 15% random moves, Hard picks the best. A hypnotized plant still uses the old zombie AI (`aiAct`). Tested with identical teams on both sides (20 battles each): Hard beat Easy 20–0, Normal beat Easy 19–0 (1 draw), Hard beat Normal 14–4 (2 draws); battles last about 15–22 rounds. Every plant move was used in versus without errors, with every gimmick, and the story save stayed byte-for-byte the same.

## How the code is organized

Plain classic scripts (not modules), loaded in this order by `index.html`. Top-level names are shared, so **names must be unique across files**:

1. `helpers.js`: `$`, `store` (localStorage with try/catch, prefix `lawnlegends:`), `pick`, `rand`, `chance`, `clamp`, `esc`, `shuffle`.
2. `config.js`: tuning numbers (sun, team size, XP curve `xpToNext`, `statScale`, damage formula constants, status strengths, Plant Food, animation delays). **Most balance changes happen here.**
3. `data.js`: `CLASSES`, `STATUS`, `MOVES` (plants and zombies) with special-move helpers, `PLANTS` (40), `ZOMBIES` (52, each with `counter`), `GIMMICKS`.
4. `world.js`: `CHAPTERS` (maps, encounters, dialogue), map tiles (`WALLS`, `TILE_STYLE`), `SHOP_PLANTS`, `ACHIEVEMENTS`.
5. `art.js`: `SPRITES` (4-line ASCII drawings; every drawing ends with a space so a final backslash can't escape the closing backtick), `BARE` (drawings once armor breaks), `spriteFor`, the title's big letters.
6. `sound.js`: `sfx` (Web Audio beeps), mute.
7. `save.js`: `progress` (owned plants, team, coins, cleared battles, chests, position, achievements, seen zombies, counters), `loadProgress`/`saveProgress`, `equipped`, `toggleMove`, `unlockPlant`, `giveXp`, `award`, `unlockText`.
8. `battle.js`: the battle state `B` and all rules: `startBattle`, rounds and turns, `useMove`, `hit` (the damage pipeline), statuses, AI, `winBattle`/`loseBattle`.
9. `render.js`: draws the battle (`render()`), only reads `B`.
10. `screens.js`: title, overworld (`MAPS`, `renderWorld`, `moveWorld`), dialog, prebattle, results, Team, Shop, Almanac, Achievements, toasts.
11. `versus.js`: versus mode: the saved settings `vs` (`loadVersus`/`saveVersus`), team and move helpers (`vsTeam`, `vsMoves`, `randomizeTeam`), side names (`vsLabel` from settings, `vsName` during a battle), the setup screen (`renderVersus`, `vsDetail`), `startVersus`, `endVersus`, `versusOvertime`, `leaveVersus`, and the versus AI (`versusAct`, `aiValue`, `aiHitValue`, `aiDamage`, `aiStatusValue`).
12. `input.js`: every click and key.
13. `main.js`: `boot()` and the `window.lawnLegends` test hook.

### Gotchas

- `index.html` is a **page fragment** (no doctype/html/head/body) because the Artifact publisher wraps it. It keeps `<meta charset="utf-8">` and the `[hidden]` rule in `style.css` so double-clicking it works.
- Adding a map tile character: put it in `WALLS` if it blocks, and in `TILE_STYLE` with a CSS class.
- Adding a zombie: stats, moves, `desc` and `counter` in `ZOMBIES`, a drawing in `SPRITES`, then use it in a wave.
- Adding a plant: `PLANTS` entry (class, stats within budget, passive, 6 `learn` entries, `unlock`), 2 signature moves in `MOVES`, a drawing in `SPRITES`, and an unlock source (story reward, rogue battle, shop price or achievement).

## Publishing (the private Artifact link)

Publish `index.html` with every other file in `files` (`style.css` and all 13 `js/*.js`, each mapped to the same path). From a new session, first `read` the artifact URL, then publish with that `url`. The Artifact tool only publishes files inside the session's working folder or its scratchpad: a session started in `C:\git\pvz-rpg` can publish directly; the first publish (from a session in `C:\git\bbcn`) copied the files into the scratchpad first.
